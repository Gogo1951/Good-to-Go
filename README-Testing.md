# Good to Go // Test Plan

Good to Go keeps you buffed, fed and stocked: macros for your best food, potions and healthstones, a Restock List that shops for you, a click-to-cast window for missing buffs, and ready-made spell macros. This time we most want you to try Buff Duty's close button and the new Solo, Party, Raid and Battleground checkboxes, and to spend extra time on WoW Forever.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Close the Buff Duty window with its X, check it stays hidden until you reload, then Shift + Left-Click the mini-map button to bring it back sooner.
2. Target a friendly NPC, such as a city guard, and click its Buff Duty icons to buff it.
3. If you played the last version, check your When to Use and When to Show choices carried over to the new Solo, Party, Raid and Battleground checkboxes.
4. Under Consumable Macros, tick only Party in Prioritize Buff Food's When to Use, and check your Food macro uses buff food in a party but not solo.
5. With no key bound to the Buff Button, check the last line of the Buff Duty window tells you where to set one.
6. On WoW Forever, check pets never show in the Buff Duty window, and that in a battleground it hides with a note saying why.
7. On a Troll on WoW Forever, turn on Use Rapid Regeneration Instead of Eating under Class & Race, and check your Food macro casts it on every press **in combat**.

## Everything Else

8. Drag the Food and Water macros from the General tab of your Macros window onto your bars, and check each moves to your next-best item as you use one up.
9. On a mage or warlock, Right-Click your Food, Water or Healthstone macro to conjure more; on a hunter or rogue, use your Feed Pet or Poisons macro.
10. In a group, click an icon in the Buff Duty window to buff a teammate who is missing it, and on WoW Forever try it **in combat**.
11. Type /restock, add an item to your list, then visit a merchant and the bank and check your bags get topped up.
12. Turn on Enable Rank Watch (not on MoP Classic), cast a low rank of one of your spells, and check you get a note in chat, **in combat** too on WoW Forever.
13. Open the Enhanced Spellbook tab in your spellbook, make a macro for a spell, and check it still works after you train a new rank.
14. Type /gtg, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button, and check each opens the options, then try both in combat and check they refuse.

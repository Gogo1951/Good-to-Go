# Good to Go // Test Plan

Good to Go keeps you buffed, fed and stocked: it builds macros for your best food, potions and spells, shows who is missing your buffs, and restocks your bags. This is its first release, so try a bit of everything, and on WoW Forever spend extra time in the Enhanced Spellbook.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## Everything Else

1. Hover the mini-map button to see your Current Food, then Right-Click it to ignore that food and Middle-Click it to clear the Ignore List.
2. Drag the Food and Water macros from the General tab of the Macros window onto your bars, and check each moves to your next-best item as you use one up.
3. Left-Click the mini-map button to turn Buff Food & Scrolls on and off, and Shift + Left-Click it to do the same for Buff Duty.
4. In a group, click an icon in the Buff Duty window to buff a teammate who is missing it, and on WoW Forever try it in combat.
5. On a hunter, mage, warlock or rogue, try each click your class's macro lists in the mini-map tooltip.
6. Open the Enhanced Spellbook from its tab in your spellbook, make a macro for a spell, and check it still works after you train a new rank.
7. Type /restock, add an item to your list, then visit a merchant and the bank and check your bags get topped up.
8. Log in on a second character on the same realm, then hover an item to see how many each character has.
9. Start a ready check while missing a buff or wearing damaged gear, and check you get a private list of what to fix.
10. Get buffed by a stranger out in the world and by a teammate during a fight, and check each one gets a thank-you, on WoW Forever too.
11. Type /gtg, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button, and check each opens the options.
12. Try both again in combat and check the options refuse to open.

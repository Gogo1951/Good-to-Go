# Good to Go // Test Plan

Good to Go keeps you buffed, fed and stocked: macros for your best food, potions and healthstones, a Restock List that shops for you, a click-to-cast window for missing buffs, and ready-made spell macros. This time we most want you to try the new mini-map menu, whispers you can word yourself, and restock reminders that say what you're low on or out of.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Hover the mini-map button to open its new menu, click each Toggle row, and check the button's own clicks still work.
2. In the mini-map menu, open your class's Macros flyout (warriors have none) and More Features, and turn a feature off and on again from there.
3. Under Restocker, set Low Means Under, then walk into an inn or a city and check the reminder counts only what you're low on or out of.
4. On the Buff Duty and Rank Watch pages, change Send Messages In, write your own whisper, and click Send Test to Me.
5. In a group, hover a Missing Party Buffs icon in the Buff Duty window, and check its menu shows each player's minutes left or Missing, and that its two rows send the ask.
6. With Whisper Group Members on in Rank Watch (not on MoP Classic), check a group member gets the opt-out offer with their second tip, and that whispering you Opt Out puts them on Ignored Players.

## Everything Else

7. Drag the Food and Water macros from the General tab of your Macros window onto your bars, and check each moves to your next-best item as you use one up.
8. On a mage or warlock, Right-Click your Food, Water or Healthstone macro to conjure more; on a hunter or rogue, use your Feed Pet or Poisons macro.
9. In a group, click an icon in the Buff Duty window to buff a teammate who is missing it, and on WoW Forever try it **in combat**.
10. Type /restock, add an item to your list, then visit a merchant and the bank and check your bags get topped up.
11. Turn on Enable Rank Watch (not on MoP Classic), cast a low rank of one of your spells, and check you get a note in chat, **in combat** too on WoW Forever.
12. Open the Enhanced Spellbook tab in your spellbook, make a macro for a spell, and check it still works after you train a new rank.
13. Type /gtg, then click GtG Options in the mini-map menu, and check each opens the options, then try both in combat and check they refuse.

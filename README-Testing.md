# Good to Go // Test Plan

Good to Go keeps you buffed, fed and stocked: macros for your best food, potions and healthstones, a Restock List that shops for you, a click-to-cast window for missing buffs, and ready-made spell macros. This time we most want you to try the Buff Duty window's new Window Opacity setting and the Cast Next Buff key.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Hover Options at the foot of the Buff Duty window, hover each Window Opacity choice to preview it, then click one to keep it; only the background should fade.
2. In a group, target a teammate and press Cast Next Buff: it should buff your target first, then target each teammate it buffs as it goes.
3. In a raid, when just one player in a group is missing a buff, check that group's folded header in the Buff Duty window offers the single buff, not the group version.
4. Close the Buff Duty window with its close button, then hover the mini-map button and check Buff Duty reads Paused, with a note on when it comes back.
5. Turn the Restocker off, then hover the mini-map button and check its Open row is gray, with a tooltip saying why.

## Everything Else

6. Drag the Food and Water macros from the General tab of your Macros window onto your bars, and check each moves to your next-best item as you use one up.
7. On a mage or warlock, Right-Click your Food, Water or Healthstone macro to conjure more; on a hunter or rogue, use your Feed Pet or Poisons macro.
8. In a group, click an icon in the Buff Duty window to buff a teammate who is missing it, and on WoW Forever try it **in combat**.
9. Type /restock, add an item to your list, then visit a merchant and the bank and check your bags get topped up.
10. Open your spellbook, click the Enhanced Spellbook tab, and drag a spell onto your bars as a ready-made macro.
11. Turn on Enable Rank Watch (not on MoP Classic), cast a low rank of one of your spells, and check you get a note in chat, **in combat** too on WoW Forever.
12. Type /gtg, then click GtG Options in the mini-map menu, and check each opens the options, then try both in combat and check they refuse.

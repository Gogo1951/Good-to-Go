# Good to Go // Test Plan

Good to Go keeps you buffed, fed and stocked: macros for your best food, potions and healthstones, a Restock List that shops for you, a click-to-cast window for missing buffs, and ready-made spell macros. This time we most want you to try buff food and scrolls in the Buff Duty window, its new Options menu, and asking your target for buffs.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Carry buff food and a scroll whose buff you're missing, then click each one's icon under Consumables in the Buff Duty window to eat or read it.
2. With buff food and plain food in your bags, check your Food macro eats the plain food, unless Eat Buff Food When Already Well Fed is on (it starts on for WoW Forever).
3. Hover Options at the foot of the Buff Duty window, then turn a buff food, a weapon buff and a switch off and on from its menu.
4. Target a player of another class who can give a buff you're missing, and click the Ask row under them to whisper them.
5. In the options, find the new Food & Drinks and Ability Alerts While Leveling pages (not on MoP Classic), and check Restocker, Rank Watch and the Readiness Report hide their settings while switched off.
6. Type /restock while something on your list is short, and hover the line under the list to see what's short and how many a merchant will buy.
7. In the Enhanced Spellbook, Right-Click a spell, set Macro Storage to General, and check the macro moves and stays on your bars.
8. On a Troll on WoW Forever, check your Food macro casts Rapid Regeneration **in combat** and your Bandage macro casts it out of combat.

## Everything Else

9. Drag the Food and Water macros from the General tab of your Macros window onto your bars, and check each moves to your next-best item as you use one up.
10. On a mage or warlock, Right-Click your Food, Water or Healthstone macro to conjure more; on a hunter or rogue, use your Feed Pet or Poisons macro.
11. In a group, click an icon in the Buff Duty window to buff a teammate who is missing it, and on WoW Forever try it **in combat**.
12. Type /restock, add an item to your list, then visit a merchant and the bank and check your bags get topped up.
13. Turn on Enable Rank Watch (not on MoP Classic), cast a low rank of one of your spells, and check you get a note in chat, **in combat** too on WoW Forever.
14. Type /gtg, then click GtG Options in the mini-map menu, and check each opens the options, then try both in combat and check they refuse.

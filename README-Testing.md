# Good to Go // Test Plan

Good to Go keeps you buffed, fed and stocked: macros for your best food, potions and healthstones, a Restock List that shops for you, a click-to-cast window for missing buffs, and ready-made spell macros. This time we most want you to try Currency Replenish, the new coins tab on the mailbox, and the new Fishing Lure macro.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. At a mailbox, click the coins tab, pick your bank character and set a Target Amount for each character, then press Replenish on the bank and check each alt gets mailed up to its amount.
2. On an alt with more gold than its Target Amount, open the coins tab at a mailbox and press Send Excess to Bank: the extra should go to your bank.
3. Tick Fishing Lure in Consumable Macros, put it on your bars from the General tab of your Macros window, and press it with a fishing pole in hand to put on your best lure.
4. In a group, Left-Click a buff under Ask for Buffs in the Buff Duty window, and check the group chat asks it as a question, like "Could someone give Katz [Arcane Intellect], please?"

## Everything Else

5. Drag the Food and Water macros from the General tab of your Macros window onto your bars, and check each moves to your next-best item as you use one up.
6. On a mage or warlock, Right-Click your Food, Water or Healthstone macro to conjure more; on a hunter or rogue, use your Feed Pet or Poisons macro.
7. In a group, click an icon in the Buff Duty window to buff a teammate who is missing it, and on WoW Forever try it **in combat**.
8. Bind Cast Next Buff in Key Bindings and press it in a group until it says "Everyone's buffed. Good to go!"
9. Type /restock, add an item to your list, then visit a merchant and the bank and check your bags get topped up.
10. Open your spellbook, click the Enhanced Spellbook tab, and drag a spell onto your bars as a ready-made macro.
11. Turn on Enable Rank Watch (not on MoP Classic), cast a low rank of one of your spells, and check you get a note in chat, **in combat** too on WoW Forever.
12. Type /gtg, then click GtG Options in the mini-map menu, and check each opens the options, then try both in combat and check they refuse.

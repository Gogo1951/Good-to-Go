# Good to Go // Test Plan

Good to Go keeps you buffed, fed and stocked: it builds macros for your best food, potions and spells, shows who is missing your buffs, and restocks your bags. This time we most want you to try the new Rank Watch, and to spend extra time on WoW Forever.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Turn on Enable Rank Watch in the options (not on MoP Classic), cast a low rank of one of your spells, and check you get a note in chat, in combat too on WoW Forever.
2. In a group, have a friend cast a low rank and check they get one friendly whisper plus their opt-out number, then type /gtg ignore and their name and check they're no longer reported.
3. Check Good to Go no longer sends thank-you whispers or emotes, and that its old Thank You macros are gone from your Macros window.
4. On a Troll on WoW Forever, turn on Use Rapid Regeneration Instead of Eating under Class & Race, and check your Food macro casts it while it's off cooldown.
5. On a rogue or shaman on WoW Forever, put an oil or stone on your weapon, and check Buff Duty still offers your poison or imbue and a ready check asks for both.
6. In a group, hover a number icon under Missing Party-Buffs and use its menu to ask your group or whisper the players who can cast it.
7. In the Enhanced Spellbook, Right-Click a spell, open Cast Sequence, click a slot then a spell on the page, and drag another spell onto the next slot.

## Everything Else

8. Hover the mini-map button and try each click its tooltip lists.
9. Drag the Food and Water macros from the General tab of the Macros window onto your bars, and check each moves to your next-best item as you use one up.
10. In a group, click an icon in the Buff Duty window to buff a teammate who is missing it, and on WoW Forever try it in combat.
11. On a mage or warlock, Right-Click your Food, Water or Healthstone macro to conjure more; on a hunter or rogue, use your Feed Pet or Poisons macro.
12. Open the Enhanced Spellbook from its tab in your spellbook, make a macro for a spell, and check it still works after you train a new rank.
13. Type /restock, add an item to your list, then visit a merchant and the bank and check your bags get topped up.
14. Get buffed by a stranger out in the world and check they show under Return the Favor, on WoW Forever too, and that a buff from a group member doesn't.
15. Type /gtg, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button, and check each opens the options, then try both in combat and check they refuse.
